==================================================

Backend

"Orders can be placed between 7:30 AM and 7:00 PM."

==================================================
23. SAME-DAY ORDER
==================================================

Same-day orders are allowed.

However:

MINIMUM SAME-DAY SERVING/DELIVERY TIME = 2 HOURS

Formula:

Minimum Serving Time
=
Order Time + 2 Hours

Example:

Order at 10:00 AM
→ Earliest serving/delivery = 12:00 PM

Order at 1:00 PM
→ Earliest serving/delivery = 3:00 PM

Order at 5:00 PM
→ Earliest serving/delivery = 7:00 PM

==================================================
24. EARLIER SERVING
==================================================

The 2-hour rule is the minimum serving/delivery time.

It does NOT mean the restaurant must wait exactly 2 hours.

If the selected food item can be prepared earlier:

The order MAY be served/delivered earlier.

Example:

Order placed:
10:00 AM

Minimum serving:
12:00 PM

But if the food is ready earlier:

Actual serving:
11:30 AM

This is allowed.

Show the customer:

"Same-day orders require a minimum serving time of 2 hours. Depending on the ordered item, your order may be served earlier."
